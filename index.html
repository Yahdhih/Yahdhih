I'm an engineering student at ENSIIE (École Nationale Supérieure d'Informatique, Évry) pursuing a Master's-level degree in Computer Science. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, high-performance computing, and mathematical optimization.

I'm drawn to the deep mathematics underlying intelligent systems — probability theory, linear algebra, convex optimization, numerical analysis — and to translating these into efficient large-scale implementations on modern hardware: CPUs, GPUs, clusters.

From designing a 64,000-core supercomputer to training CNN-based object detection models, to building parallel algorithms with MPI and CUDA — I work across the full stack from theory to silicon.
